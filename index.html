Statement of Faith

The scriptures of the Old and New Testament are verbally inspired of God and inerrant in the original writings, and they are the supreme and final authority in faith and life. There is one God, eternally existing in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Jesus Christ was conceived by the Holy Spirit, was born of the virgin Mary, was sinless, and is true God and true Man.
