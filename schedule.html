Schedule of Meetings

All times are Eastern Time (Florida).

Sundays — The Lord’s Day

  • 10:30 – 11:30 AM — Worship / Breaking of Bread Service The Lord’s Supper, remembered as the Savior requested of His own (Luke 22:19–20; 1 Corinthians 11:23–26).
  • 11:45 AM – 12:30 PM — Singing and Ministry from the Word of God Ministry by brothers in Christ or other visiting ministers (Acts 2:42), followed by Sunday School lessons for the family.
  • Every Second Sunday of the Month — Gospel Program After the Breaking of Bread meeting there is a special time of singing and Gospel outreach at 11:45 AM, followed by a meal for all present (Romans 1:16).

Tuesdays

  • 12:00 – 1:00 PM — Intercessory Prayer (Zoom) Brothers in Christ pray for the various requests of anyone who is in need of prayer (Philippians 4:6–7).

Thursdays

  • 7:00 – 8:30 PM — Prayer & Bible Study or Ministry (Zoom)
