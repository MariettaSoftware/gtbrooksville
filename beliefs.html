What We Believe

Statement of Faith

We believe in the Trinity consisting of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. We believe in the entire Bible as the inspired Word of God which is pure, powerful, and permanent (Psalm 12:6; Hebrews 4:12; Psalm 119:89). We believe that all people are sinners and in need of the Savior. We believe in the virgin birth of the Lord Jesus Christ, who is truly Man and truly God, and the only Savior of sinners.

We believe that the Lord Jesus Christ loves us and died on the cross at Calvary for our sins, that He was buried and rose again on the third day according to the Scriptures (1 Corinthians 15:3–4). All who accept Him as their personal Lord and Savior by grace through faith in Him have been saved (Acts 16:31; Ephesians 2:8). He is the only saving name and sure way to the Father’s heart and heaven (John 14:6; Acts 4:12). On believing on Him the believer is indwelt and sealed by the Holy Spirit (Ephesians 1:13). Anyone who rejects Him will be lost and face judgment (Hebrews 9:27).

We believe in the convicting, regenerating, and guiding power of the Holy Spirit, and the responsibility of the believer to walk in the Spirit according to God’s holiness.

We believe that the Church of Jesus Christ is one body composed of all born-again believers; that Christ in Heaven is its only Head; that the Holy Spirit is present in the believers here on earth; and that our gathering-center for worship on Sundays is in the name of Jesus Christ. We believe in the ordinances of water baptism and the Lord’s Supper. We believe in the bodily resurrection of the believer at the coming of the Lord Jesus Christ for His Church. We believe in the imminent coming of the Lord Jesus Christ for His Church and to set up His kingdom in righteousness.

“Yet in all these things we are more than conquerors through Him who loved us. For I am persuaded that neither death nor life, nor angels nor principalities nor powers, nor things present nor things to come, nor height nor depth, nor any other created thing, shall be able to separate us from the love of God which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.” Romans 8:37–39

Practices

The Lord’s Table

If you enter the modest place of Christians gathered together unto the name of Jesus, you will see a table upon which is bread and a container of wine. The purpose of this service is an honest effort to fulfill the request of the Savior made on the night of His betrayal, “This do in remembrance of me,” and to carry out the instructions given by revelation to the apostle Paul as to observance of the Lord’s supper (Luke 22:14–20; 1 Corinthians 11:23–29).

The endeavor is to follow the example of the early Christians who “continued steadfastly in the apostles’ doctrine and fellowship, and in breaking of bread, and prayers” and “upon the first day of the week…came together to break bread” (Acts 2:42; 20:7).

The privilege of partaking of the Lord’s supper also conveys a responsibility to “guard” the table of the Lord in a manner that shows the holy character of Him whose death is commemorated (1 Corinthians 11:27–34). All those who partake in this privilege should be in unity of purpose, mind, and spirit of which the Lord has commanded to this fellowship.

Worship Meeting

These Christians follow the leading of the Holy Spirit. Believing that the Spirit of God divides “to everyone individually as He wills” (1 Corinthians 12:11), any godly brother not under any discipline may name a hymn to be sung by all, lead in prayer, read Scripture and give thanks for the bread and cup in participation of the Lord’s supper.

In obedience to the divine injunction, the brothers should not have their heads covered (e.g., hat, cap, etc.) and sisters should cover man’s glory by wearing a head covering in order not to offend God. In recognition of the order of God’s house, while the brothers have the responsibility to lead audibly, the sisters have the responsibility to support the assembly (1 Corinthians 11:3–13; 14:34).

Gospel Outreach Services

Every second Sunday of the month, after the Breaking of Bread meeting, there is a special time of singing and Gospel outreach, followed by a meal for all present (Romans 1:16).

Non-Denominational

Brethren Assemblies can be found in many parts of the world where members gather simply to the Name of the Lord Jesus Christ. We have no church organization, headquarters, presiding bishops, appointed elders, or ordained clergy. Yet there is no independency. We function together, “endeavoring to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace” (Ephesians 4:3). We believe that each local Assembly is a representation of the whole Body of Christ and recognize its actions in the name of the Lord and according to God’s Word as authoritative and binding everywhere (Matthew 18:18).

“…Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and you will be saved…” Acts 16:31

A welcome is extended to anyone interested in hearing the Good News of God’s saving grace and ministry of the Word of God.
